# August Blog Entry Design

## Goal

Add one new blog post dated August 30, 2026 to the Daats website, including:
- a new article card on the homepage and blog index
- a new generated hero image saved in the project
- a full article page with content length aligned to the existing blog posts

## Recommended Topic

Use an evergreen late-August freight topic:

**How Shippers Should Prepare for Fall Freight Before Peak Season Hits**

This fits the calendar, matches the practical Daats voice, and stays useful after publication.

## Content Design

The article should follow the existing Daats article pattern:
- category label in the existing blog taxonomy
- title, short deck, and `AUG 30, 2026 - 4 MIN READ` metadata
- one hero image above the article body
- 5 to 6 section headings
- practical shipper guidance with direct, operational language
- no mid-article images

Target article body length:
- at least 800 words
- at most 1600 words

Recommended category:
- `Industry News`

Reasoning:
- it fits seasonal freight planning without requiring hard news claims
- it matches the site's existing shipper-advice positioning

## Image Design

Create one new blog hero image with the same visual class as the current blog images:
- logistics/trucking marketing image
- professional, realistic, late-summer freight setting
- no logos or brand marks
- modern 2025-style truck fronts
- composition suitable for both the blog index card and article hero

Suggested scene:
- a mixed-fleet line of modern trucks at a distribution yard during late-afternoon light, with visible dock activity and a clean commercial look

Save path:
- `assets/images/blog-fall-freight-prep.png`

## Implementation Design

Update the generated site from the source-of-truth article list in `scripts/build-site.mjs`.

Required changes:
- add a new article object to `articles`
- place it first in the array so it appears first in blog listings
- reference the new image asset
- regenerate the static HTML outputs
- ensure article pagination still works with the new ordering

Expected generated outputs:
- `index.html`
- `blog.html`
- `blog/fall-freight-prep-before-peak-season.html`

## Testing Design

Update the site contract tests so they continue to validate:
- the expected set of blog images now includes the new image
- the new article page is part of the required generated pages
- the article still satisfies the existing word-count and single-hero-image constraints

Verification steps:
- run `npm run build`
- run `npm test`

## Non-Goals

- no changes to the diesel pricing section
- no changes to existing article copy beyond ordering/pagination generated by the new article
- no CMS or dynamic blog system changes

## Risks And Handling

Risk:
- the new post could drift away from the existing Daats tone

Handling:
- keep the article operational, shipper-focused, and free of buzzword-heavy language

Risk:
- the new image could feel inconsistent with the existing blog art

Handling:
- keep the same realistic marketing-photo feel and use a wide hero composition
